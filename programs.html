The programs, in detail

Four programs of 8 to 12 weeks, cooked on your own smoker in your own yard.

Program 01 · Everything starts here

Fire & temperature

Most barbecue problems are temperature problems. We work on fuel and airflow for your cooker, holding a steady 250 for hours, recognizing clean smoke from dirty, and placing probes where they measure something useful — never the lid thermometer.

  • 8 weeks
  • Any cooker
  • One cook a week
  • Two probe thermometers

Program 02 · The big three

Brisket, pork & ribs

We cook each one properly, in order of difficulty. Trimming that actually helps, when to wrap and in what, cooking to feel rather than to a number, and resting long enough — which is where most home cooks lose the juice they spent twelve hours protecting.

  • 12 weeks
  • Fire control sorted
  • One cook a week
  • A cooler for resting

Start this program

Program 03 · Stop buying bottles

Rubs, sauces & sides

Commercial rubs are mostly salt, sugar and filler. We build rubs from scratch, get salt right for the cut and the cook length, make sauces balanced against the meat rather than drowning it, and cover the handful of sides that actually matter.

  • 8 weeks
  • Any level
  • 2 hours a week
  • A scale

Program 04 · Ready when they arrive

Cooking for a crowd

Cooking for twenty is a logistics problem. We plan backwards from serving time, build in the buffer that a stall demands, cover holding food safely at temperature, and set up a cook schedule that lets you sleep and still serve on time.

  • 8 weeks
  • Confident on the big three
  • One cook a week
  • A calendar
